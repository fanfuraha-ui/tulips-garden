About us

Karibuni to Tulips Garden

Tulips Garden is a green escape just off the road in Gikambura, Kikuyu. We serve hearty Kenyan favourites from the grill and the pan, keep a clean pool for swimmers big and small, and host the celebrations that families remember. Whether it's a quiet lunch, a Sunday funday or a baby shower, we'll make it special.
